# PFC System - Client Discovery Questions

## Critical Discovery Questions

Use this checklist during your initial client meeting to gather essential information.

---

## 1. Current State Assessment

### Existing Systems
- [ ] What CRM or ticketing system do you currently use, if any?
- [ ] What is "Follow-Up Pathway"? (Existing platform or new name?)
  - [ ] Do they have API documentation?
  - [ ] What features does it currently offer?
  - [ ] What's missing that you need?
- [ ] What email system do you use? (Gmail, Office 365, custom)
- [ ] What phone system? (Landlines, VoIP, specific provider)
- [ ] How do you currently track invoices and payments?
- [ ] Do you use any project management tools? (Asana, Monday, Trello)

### Current Workflow Pain Points
- [ ] How do you currently receive job requests?
  - [ ] What percentage from each channel? (email/phone/web/sms)
- [ ] How are requests currently documented?
  - [ ] Email threads?
  - [ ] Spreadsheets?
  - [ ] Written notes?
  - [ ] Mix of all above?
- [ ] What percentage of requests fall through the cracks?
- [ ] How long does it typically take to respond to a request?
- [ ] Where do most bottlenecks occur?
- [ ] What tasks most commonly get delayed?

### Team Structure
- [ ] How many people will use this system?
  - [ ] Admin/Executives: ___
  - [ ] Staffing Department: ___
  - [ ] Logistics Department: ___
  - [ ] Finance Department: ___
  - [ ] Scheduling Department: ___
  - [ ] Customer Service Reps: ___
- [ ] What are the department reporting structures?
- [ ] Who are the key stakeholders who need real-time visibility?
- [ ] Who is "Marco" (mentioned in escalations)?

---

## 2. Volume & Scale

### Request Volume
- [ ] How many job requests do you receive per month?
  - [ ] Average: ___
  - [ ] Peak season: ___
  - [ ] Slow season: ___
- [ ] How many are handled simultaneously?
- [ ] What's the typical lifecycle of a job (start to finish)?
  - [ ] Fastest: ___
  - [ ] Average: ___
  - [ ] Longest: ___
- [ ] How many clients are active at any given time?

### Growth Projections
- [ ] Expected growth over next 12 months?
- [ ] Expected growth over next 3 years?
- [ ] Any upcoming business changes that could affect volume?

---

## 3. Business Requirements

### Job Types
- [ ] What are the main categories of jobs you handle?
  1. _______________
  2. _______________
  3. _______________
  4. _______________
  5. _______________
- [ ] Do different job types require different workflows?
- [ ] Are there standard packages/templates for common jobs?

### Client Interaction
- [ ] Do clients need to see ticket status in real-time?
- [ ] Should clients receive automated updates?
  - [ ] At what milestones?
- [ ] Do clients need to upload documents?
- [ ] Do clients need to approve plans before execution?
- [ ] Should clients be able to modify requests after submission?

### Department Workflows
**Staffing:**
- [ ] How do you currently match staff to jobs?
- [ ] Do you have a pool of available staff in a database?
- [ ] What information is needed to fulfill a staffing request?
- [ ] Average time to confirm staffing?

**Logistics:**
- [ ] What does logistics involve? (Transportation, equipment, setup?)
- [ ] Dependencies on other departments?
- [ ] External vendors involved?

**Finance:**
- [ ] When are quotes/estimates provided?
- [ ] Who approves budgets?
- [ ] Integration with accounting software needed?
- [ ] Payment terms and tracking requirements?

**Scheduling:**
- [ ] Do you use a shared calendar?
- [ ] How far in advance are jobs typically scheduled?
- [ ] How do you handle scheduling conflicts?

---

## 4. AI & Automation Preferences

### AI-Powered Assignment
- [ ] Are you comfortable with AI making initial department assignments?
- [ ] Should there always be a human review step?
- [ ] What happens when AI can't confidently categorize a request?
- [ ] Should AI learn from corrections over time?

### Automation Boundaries
- [ ] What should be fully automated?
- [ ] What requires human approval?
- [ ] What should never be automated?

---

## 5. Notification & Escalation

### Notification Preferences
- [ ] Which channels for notifications?
  - [ ] Email
  - [ ] SMS
  - [ ] Phone calls
  - [ ] In-app notifications
  - [ ] Push notifications (mobile)
- [ ] Are the proposed escalation times appropriate?
  - [ ] Level 1 (2 hours)
  - [ ] Level 2 (4 hours)
  - [ ] Level 3 (8 hours)
  - [ ] Level 4 (24 hours)
- [ ] Should escalation times vary by job priority?
- [ ] Should there be "quiet hours" for non-urgent notifications?

### Escalation Hierarchy
- [ ] Confirm escalation path for each department
- [ ] Who should receive critical overdue alerts?
- [ ] Should escalations be different for different job types?

---

## 6. Reporting & Analytics

### Required Reports
- [ ] What reports do you need daily?
- [ ] What reports do you need weekly?
- [ ] What reports do you need monthly?
- [ ] What metrics matter most to your business?
  - [ ] Revenue
  - [ ] Client satisfaction
  - [ ] Staff utilization
  - [ ] Response times
  - [ ] Completion rates
  - [ ] Other: _______________

### Dashboard Viewers
- [ ] Who needs access to executive dashboard?
- [ ] Should different roles see different dashboards?
- [ ] Do you need to export data regularly?
  - [ ] For what purpose?
  - [ ] What format? (Excel, CSV, PDF)

---

## 7. Integration Requirements

### Must-Have Integrations
- [ ] Accounting software (QuickBooks, Xero, other?)
- [ ] Existing calendar systems (Google Calendar, Outlook)
- [ ] Payroll systems
- [ ] Document storage (Google Drive, Dropbox, SharePoint)
- [ ] Communication tools (Slack, Microsoft Teams)

### Nice-to-Have Integrations
- [ ] Social media (for intake?)
- [ ] E-signature (DocuSign, HelloSign)
- [ ] Background check services (for staffing)
- [ ] GPS/Location tracking (for logistics)

---

## 8. Compliance & Security

### Regulatory Requirements
- [ ] Industry-specific regulations?
- [ ] Data privacy requirements (GDPR, CCPA)?
- [ ] Financial compliance (PCI if handling payments)?
- [ ] Background check record retention requirements?
- [ ] Audit trail requirements?

### Security Requirements
- [ ] Who should have access to what data?
- [ ] Role-based permissions needed?
- [ ] Multi-factor authentication required?
- [ ] Data retention policy?
- [ ] Data backup requirements?

---

## 9. Budget & Timeline

### Budget
- [ ] What's the budget range for development?
  - [ ] Under $50k
  - [ ] $50k - $100k
  - [ ] $100k - $200k
  - [ ] $200k+
  - [ ] Not yet determined
- [ ] What's the acceptable monthly operating cost?
  - [ ] Under $500
  - [ ] $500 - $1,000
  - [ ] $1,000 - $2,500
  - [ ] $2,500+

### Timeline
- [ ] When do you need this system live?
  - [ ] ASAP
  - [ ] 3 months
  - [ ] 6 months
  - [ ] 12 months
  - [ ] Flexible
- [ ] Are there business deadlines driving this?
  - [ ] Busy season approaching?
  - [ ] Contract obligations?
  - [ ] Competitive pressure?

### Phased Rollout
- [ ] Can this be launched in phases?
- [ ] What's the absolute minimum viable product?
- [ ] Which departments could start using it first?

---

## 10. Success Criteria

### How will you measure success?
- [ ] Faster response times
- [ ] Fewer missed requests
- [ ] Higher client satisfaction
- [ ] Improved staff utilization
- [ ] Better communication
- [ ] Increased revenue
- [ ] Time savings (how much?)
- [ ] Other: _______________

### What would make this project a failure?
- [ ] Too complex for staff to use
- [ ] Doesn't integrate with critical systems
- [ ] Too slow/unreliable
- [ ] Costs too much to operate
- [ ] Other: _______________

---

## 11. User Adoption

### Training
- [ ] What's your staff's technical comfort level?
  - [ ] Very tech-savvy
  - [ ] Moderate
  - [ ] Limited
- [ ] How much training time is available?
- [ ] Preferred training format?
  - [ ] In-person
  - [ ] Video tutorials
  - [ ] Documentation
  - [ ] Mix of all

### Change Management
- [ ] How do you typically roll out new systems?
- [ ] Who are the champions who will advocate for this?
- [ ] Who might resist the change?
- [ ] What concerns do staff have about new systems?

---

## 12. Mobile Requirements

### Mobile Access
- [ ] Do staff need mobile access?
  - [ ] If yes, which staff?
- [ ] Native apps needed or mobile web sufficient?
- [ ] What features are critical on mobile?
  - [ ] View tickets
  - [ ] Update status
  - [ ] Communicate with clients
  - [ ] Receive notifications
  - [ ] Upload photos/documents
- [ ] iOS, Android, or both?

---

## 13. Future Considerations

### Future Features
- [ ] What features aren't critical now but might be later?
- [ ] Plans for expanding to new services?
- [ ] Plans for new locations/offices?
- [ ] Multi-company support needed?

### Technology Preferences
- [ ] Cloud vs. on-premise preference?
- [ ] Preferred cloud provider (AWS, Google, Azure)?
- [ ] Open source vs. proprietary?
- [ ] Any technologies to avoid?

## Red Flags to Watch For

- [ ] Unclear decision-making authority
- [ ] Unrealistic timeline expectations
- [ ] Budget not aligned with requirements
- [ ] Resistance to process changes
- [ ] Critical integrations not possible
- [ ] Scope creep without budget flexibility
- [ ] Lack of internal technical resources for maintenance

---

## Green Lights

- [ ] Clear pain points that system will solve
- [ ] Engaged stakeholders across departments
- [ ] Realistic budget and timeline
- [ ] Willingness to adapt processes
- [ ] Strong executive sponsorship
- [ ] Clear success metrics defined
- [ ] Technical resources available for support
