2. Detailed Intake Workflows

Four distinct intake channels ensure clients can submit requests through their preferred method. Each channel automatically creates a structured ticket in Follow-Up Pathway.

Intake Channels:

  • Email: Automatic parsing extracts key information and creates ticket
  • Phone: Rep-assisted intake with call recording attached to ticket
  • Web Form: Self-service portal with instant confirmation
  • SMS: Quick text-to-ticket with automated acknowledgment

3. AI-Powered Auto-Assignment Logic

Advanced AI integration analyzes each ticket and intelligently determines which departments need to be involved, what resources are required, and how to prioritize the work.

AI Capabilities:

  • Natural language processing of ticket descriptions
  • Automatic identification of required departments
  • Extraction of key details (staffing numbers, dates, budget hints)
  • Priority assignment based on urgency indicators
  • Historical pattern recognition for similar job types

5. Escalation & Notification System

A comprehensive four-level escalation system ensures no task falls through the cracks. Executives receive real-time visibility into critical issues while team members get timely reminders.

Escalation Levels:

  • Level 1 (2 hours): Gentle reminder to assigned person
  • Level 2 (4 hours): Team lead notification
  • Level 3 (8 hours): Department manager alert
  • Level 4 (24 hours): Executive team (Marco) notification

All communication—internal, with clients, and cross-departmental—is centralized within each ticket, creating a complete audit trail and eliminating information silos.

Communication Features:

  • @Mentions trigger immediate notifications
  • Multi-channel client communication (email, SMS, phone, portal)
  • Automatic logging of all interactions
  • Document sharing with granular permissions
  • Cross-department handoff notifications
